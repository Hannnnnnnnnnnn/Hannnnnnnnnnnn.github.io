03

KITS Black Friday

Campaign design for the most competitive retail week of the year — hero banners, email and on-site merchandising built as one modular system.

Role
Graphic Design
Period
Black Friday & Cyber Monday week
Client
KITS
Tools
Veo 3.1 (motion)

Problem

Black Friday is the week every eyewear brand shouts at once. The campaign had to cut through to digital-native consumers while marketing spend stayed pinned at roughly 14.3% of revenue — so the creative had to work across thousands of touchpoints without a budget to match.

Approach

Cyberpunk motion instead of static imagery. Neon purple typography over floating product layers, built to move rather than sit still, so the same idea carried across social and web.

One modular system, three surfaces. Hero banners sized for desktop, tablet and mobile; “The Trigger” mobile-first email designed for clarity and urgency, carrying the neon motif into the inbox so the jump to the site felt seamless; and “The Sustainer” PLP banners placed between product grids as visual breakers, re-cut into horizontal layouts that highlight a category without interrupting browsing.

Static assets turned into video. Motion was generated with Veo 3.1, tuned toward natural head movement and blinking to keep it clear of the uncanny valley.

campaign visuals

Result

The campaign produced a record week: $5.8M revenue in a single week and over 16,900 pairs of glasses ordered. Glasses revenue in November grew 56% year over year, and the business passed a $300 million revenue run rate for the first time. Repeat customers accounted for 65% of revenue contribution.

“This was a breakthrough week for KITS and for our mission to build a leading consumer eyecare brand in North America.”

Roger Hardy, Co-Founder and CEO, KITS
